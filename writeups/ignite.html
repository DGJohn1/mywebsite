Enumeration

Looks like we've got a FuelCMS instance running on port 80. A little subdomain enumeration lets us bypass the disallowed entry.

Going to any of the directories after /fuel/ sends us to the login page. Luckily, the home page on port 80 gives us the default login credentials.

After inputting the credentials, we're sent to the configuration page.

Searching around and failing to upload any reverse shells directly, I look into FuelCMS vulnerabilities instead. Sure enough, there's a shell spawner exploit for version 1.4.
